Project Overview

This project involved the design and development of a private, local-first AI automation system for a UK-based business.

The objective was to automate customer communication, booking-related workflows, system monitoring and selected internal operational tasks while maintaining greater control over business data and infrastructure.

The platform runs on dedicated Mac Mini infrastructure and uses specialized AI agents, local language models and communication integrations to support daily operations.

The Challenge

The client needed a more reliable way to manage recurring operational tasks, customer communication and system monitoring.

Several processes required manual attention, including responding to customer enquiries, handling missed calls, supporting bookings, monitoring system health and checking whether communication services remained connected.

The solution also needed to prioritize privacy, reliability and human oversight rather than depending entirely on external cloud-based AI services.

The Solution

I designed and implemented a modular AI automation architecture using multiple specialized agents, local language models and communication integrations.

Each agent was assigned a defined operational responsibility, with structured handoffs, logging and safety controls. The system connected customer communication channels, booking workflows, monitoring services and internal automation into a single operational environment.

Core Features

Multi-Agent Architecture

Specialized agents for operational tasks, monitoring, reporting and workflow coordination.

Local AI Model Deployment

Local language models through Ollama for privacy, control and predictable operation.

WhatsApp Automation

Customer communication and automated response workflows through WhatsApp integration.

Twilio Call & SMS Workflows

UK voice and SMS functionality with missed-call handling and follow-up communication.

Booking Assistance

Automated support for booking interactions and operational follow-ups.

System Monitoring

Health, memory, service connectivity and operational condition monitoring.

Alerts & Recovery

Alerts and recovery-oriented processes for disconnected services or constrained resources.

Structured Agent Handoffs

Structured data, activity logs and idempotent workflow controls between agents.

Human Approval Controls

Sensitive actions remain under human control through approval rules and safe modes.

Technical Logging

Structured JSONL records for actions, errors, system activity and workflow outcomes.

Automation Flow

Customer communication
Customer Call or WhatsApp Message→Communication Gateway→AI Agent Analysis→Booking or Support Workflow→Customer Response→Internal Logging→Monitoring & Human Escalation
Internal monitoring
System Health Check→Issue Detected→Monitoring Agent→Alert Generated→Recovery or Human Review→Status Logged

Outcome

The resulting system provided the client with a more structured and automated operating environment for communication, booking support and infrastructure monitoring.

It reduced reliance on repeatedly checking services manually and created a foundation for expanding additional AI-assisted business workflows.

The modular architecture also made it possible to improve individual agents, integrations and automation processes without rebuilding the entire platform.
